BONGOMAN ebook art
==================

This folder REPLACES public/images/ebooks/ whole. Delete the old one and
drop this in. The ebook-01 to ebook-11 numbering is gone: nothing points
at those files any more.

22 files, flat:

  <title-slug>.png            600 x 900   cover, matches the card's 2:3
  <title-slug>-backdrop.png   1600 x 800  the big art behind the row, 2:1

EbookCatalogue.jsx builds both filenames from the book's title, so if you
rename a book in that file you rename its two images to match and nothing
else changes.

REPLACING THEM
--------------

Covers: the card lays a tag and a "Coming soon" line over the bottom, with
a scrim behind them. Keep important detail clear of the bottom third. The
lettered title is hidden automatically whenever a cover image exists,
because a real cover carries its own title.

Backdrops: these sit behind the card row at 55 percent opacity with cards
in front of them. They read as atmosphere, so low contrast and a clear
centre work better than a busy composition.

If the real covers are scans of the printed books rather than new art,
their ratio will not be 2:3. Say so and the card geometry gets set to the
real ratio once, rather than every cover being cropped slightly forever.
