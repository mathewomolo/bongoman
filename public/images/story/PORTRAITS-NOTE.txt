REPLACING THEM
--------------

Square, and displayed around 175px wide, so 350 is already a 2x source.
Real photographs are worth more here than illustrations: this section is
the claim that two actual people made this, and a photograph is the
evidence.

The portrait overhangs the top corner of its panel, so it is CROPPED to a
square with `object-fit: cover`. Frame the face centrally and leave a
little air around it, or the crop will take an ear.

They mirror each other: Kham's sits over the top left of the left panel,
Mathew's over the top right of the right panel. Shoot or crop them with
the same framing distance so the pair reads as one set rather than two
unrelated pictures.
